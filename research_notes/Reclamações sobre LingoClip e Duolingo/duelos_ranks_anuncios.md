# Competitive social features (ranks, leagues, friend duels) and ads in free quiz/trivia/learning games: what players love, hate and abandon

Research date: 2026-10-21. Every source is dated where the date could be confirmed. **[OLD]** marks material from before 2023. **[UNVERIFIED]** marks figures that came from search-result summaries or vendor pages I could not check against a primary source. Most user-sentiment evidence below is anecdotal (store reviews, forums, parent reviews). Most quantitative ad data comes from ad-tech vendors with a commercial interest.

---

## 1. Duels, async challenges, matchmaking, ranks/ELO, leagues and leaderboards in reference games: what users love and hate, and why they churn

### Takeaway
Players like competition when it is (a) against friends or against people at a similar level and activity, (b) quick to start, and (c) optional. It turns toxic or gets abandoned when the leaderboard rewards grinding or cheating (Duolingo XP farming and bots), when the rating feels like a verdict on the player (chess "rating anxiety"), when ads interrupt timed play (Perguntados), or when the content or opponent pool stagnates. QuizUp did not die because the duels failed. It died of monetization: weak revenue, a failed TV bet, then years in maintenance mode.

### Cited Findings

**Duolingo leagues, friend quests and friend streaks**
- League design, from Duolingo's blog (May 3, 2023):
  - 10 leagues, with Diamond at the top.
  - Each week, learners are matched with people who have "similar study habits" and are in a similar time zone. Groups mix courses.
  - Leagues reset weekly on Sunday in local time.
  - The top 10 in Diamond can enter a three-round Diamond Tournament, which does not run every week.
  - Opt-out: turn off "Make My Profile Public" in web settings.
  - Duolingo removes learners with "illegitimate XP gains" and says cheating is rare.
  - The only evidence given is qualitative: 2018 tests showed "a little competition worked for a *lot* of learners."
  — [Duolingo Blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-leagues-leaderboards/)
- Burnout and churn: a developer quit Duolingo after a **1,541-day streak**. He described the last months as a chore, driven by the morning and evening calls to earn the "early bird" and "night owl" bonuses and the weekly XP targets needed to stay in the top league (May 25, 2025). — [MBS blog](https://www.mbsplugins.de/archive/2025-05-25/I_quit_Duolingo)
- Gaming the leaderboard: a MetaFilter commenter stopped using the app because leagues seemed to encourage botting, or repeating a very short intro lesson to climb (undated). — [MetaFilter](https://www.metafilter.com/199369/I-Am-Going-Out-In-A-Blaze-Of-Glory)
- A fan newsletter says Diamond league "can push people to take shortcuts" to maximize XP. Another post says a later change to how XP is awarded let learners make "healthy progress" and still do well, though only in some courses (undated). — [Duoplanet](https://newsletter.duoplanet.com/posts/duolingo-league-easier); [Duoplanet: XP](https://newsletter.duoplanet.com/posts/has-duolingo-finally-fixed-xp)
- A Yahoo News article on cheating in Duolingo leagues said suspicion of implausible XP is common and "can be discouraging to further learning, or it can prompt a kind of arms race." The URL now returns 404, so I am relying on the search snippet. **[UNVERIFIED]** — [Yahoo (dead link)](https://malaysia.news.yahoo.com/duolingo-app-language-learning-why-165500197.html)

  - Learners with at least one Friend Streak are **22% more likely** to complete their daily lesson, and the likelihood rises with more streaks.

**Chess.com / Lichess ratings**
- Coaching sources describe "rating anxiety" as the most common reason players, especially new ones, avoid rated games. They keep doing puzzles and openings but stop playing. The number feels like a verdict on "whether you are smart," and some players quit after a win to protect their points. This is anecdotal coaching material. — [ChessWorld](https://www.chessworld.net/what-is-rating-anxiety-in-chess.asp); [ChessWorld: afraid to play rated](https://www.chessworld.net/why-am-i-afraid-to-play-rated-chess.asp); [Lichess blog: rating fear](https://lichess.org/@/xxonx/blog/how-to-deal-with-rating-fear/tIJK9w4I)

- Chess.com Daily (async) has to police stalling:

  - "Vacation abuse" and stalling break the rules.
  - Tournaments by default exclude players who timed out in more than 10% of daily games over the last 90 days.
  - In a survey, Chess.com acknowledged "regular reports of vacation abuse."

### Inferences
- Rank anxiety and leaderboard cheating are the two main ways competitive features turn toxic. A song game can design against both:
  - Keep friend duels **unrated** or low-stakes by default.
  - Put ranked play in weekly leagues matched by activity level and time zone, as Duolingo does.
  - Offer a private or opt-out mode.
  - Cap or diminish XP from replaying the same song, so leagues reward learning rather than farming the shortest track.
- In a lyrics gap-fill duel, cheating is easier than in general trivia, because lyrics can be googled in seconds. Possible guards:
  - Tight per-blank timers.
  - Audio-driven prompts that need listening.
  - Randomized blank selection per player.
  - Scoring on speed plus accuracy.
  - No answer reveal until both players finish.
- Ads inside timed rounds are a specific, documented Brazilian complaint (Perguntados Triviathon). Never place ads between questions or blanks of a round.
- QuizUp shows that strong engagement with real-time quiz duels does not by itself make a business. Monetization has to be designed in from the start. A dependency on a large live player pool (instant matchmaking) also weakens as the pool shrinks or skews to one region.
- Duolingo's evidence favors **light, low-coordination social features** (shared streaks, a cap of 5, nudges) over heavy head-to-head features for daily habit.

- I found no official Etermax data on duel churn, cheating, or opponent no-shows, and no Reclame Aqui complaint threads specifically about Perguntados. Searches returned unrelated companies.

## 2. Async vs real-time duels: which retains better among friends with different schedules, and the problem of opponents never finishing turns

### Takeaway
I found no rigorous public dataset comparing retention of async and real-time duels. The structural evidence favors async for friends on different schedules: mobile use is intermittent, and async scales to many parallel games. Every async system then has to fight stalling and abandonment, using deadlines, timeouts, vacation limits and penalties. Real-time works best as a scheduled or room-based "party" event shared by link (Gartic, Kahoot, Quizlet Live), not as a matchmaking dependency.

### Cited Findings
- A game-dev forum practitioner says synchronous play "is a lot harder" on mobile "because most people use their phones intermittently." Async forces designers to decide how long to wait for an opponent. — [GameDev.net forum](https://gamedev.net/forums/topic/668722-turn-taking-instant-or-notify/5232647?page=1)
- An April 2025 blog post claims "one research study" found 30% higher retention for async over six months. The study is unnamed and the post is promotional. **[UNVERIFIED]** — [Wayline](https://www.wayline.io/blog/asynchronous-multiplayer-reclaiming-time-mobile-gaming)
- Async lets you leave after your move and get notified when it's your turn. Words With Friends allowed up to 20 simultaneous games, so you aren't bored when one opponent "decides to go to work or to bed." It also showed an "eyes" indicator when the opponent was in the game. **[OLD, 2010–2013]** — [Giant Bomb: Async Multiplayer](https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Concepts/Asynchronous_Multiplayer); [MobyGames: WWF](https://www.mobygames.com/game/45618)
- Stalling is a known async failure: a 2013 forum commenter noted that async games often stall because people "just give up if they're losing." **[OLD]** — [TouchArcade forum](https://toucharcade.com/community/threads/wizard-ops-tactics-needs-you-async-multiplayer-turn-based-strategy-game.184817/)
- Chess.com's async (Daily) mode needs a whole policy layer (current support pages):
  - Days-long move deadlines and timeouts.
  - Vacation days, capped and applied to all games.
  - Anti-stalling rules.
  - Exclusion from tournaments for players with more than 10% timeouts.
  - A setting to claim a win instantly when an opponent times out.
  Users still report timeouts and "vacation abuse" damaging tournaments. — [Chess.com Support: timeouts](https://support.chess.com/article/319-what-are-timeouts-why-cant-i-play-in-tournaments-because-of-them); [Chess.com Support: stalling](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8584203-what-is-stalling); [Chess.com vacation survey](https://www.chess.com/announcements/view/chess-com-vacation-time-survey-2); [Chess.com forum: USTCL timeouts](https://www.chess.com/clubs/forum/view/timing-out-on-ustcl-games)
- Real-time quiz depends on a live pool. QuizUp's fast matching in 2014 was credited to its large, mostly American player base. **[OLD]** — [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/QuizUp-game-review-mercilessly-addictive_id54171); [Irish Times](https://irishtimes.com/business/technology/quizup-1.1601319)
- Real-time works well when a host convenes the group: Gartic Phone rooms are shared by link, and Kahoot and Quizlet Live are joined by code in class. — [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/games/como-jogar-gartic-phone-telefone-sem-fio/); [Quizlet Help](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/articles/360030985431)
- Duolingo designed Friend Streak as non-interactive, so friends need not be online together. Shared progress is lost if the friend skips the day. — [Duolingo Blog 2024](https://blog.duolingo.com/product-lessons-friend-streak/); [Duolingo Blog 2023](https://blog.duolingo.com/friends-social-features/)

### Inferences
- A sensible default is **async "challenge a friend"**:
  - Player A plays a song round and gets a score.
  - Player B gets a WhatsApp link and plays the same round within a deadline, such as 24–72h.
  - Show the result only after both have played.
  This avoids "waiting on turns" altogether: each side plays a complete round, unlike the move-by-move Words With Friends model. Stalling then means "never started," which a deadline and auto-expiry handle cleanly.
- Add **real-time** as an optional "sala ao vivo" (live room) opened by link, Gartic-style, for friends who are already chatting. Do not depend on random-matchmaking liquidity in the early days.
- Allow multiple concurrent challenges. If a friend never responds, offer fallbacks so the player is never stuck: "play against their ghost or best score," or "re-send to another friend."
- Expired challenges should not count against rating or streak, to avoid punishing the challenger.

### Gaps
- I found no public A/B or cohort data comparing async vs real-time retention in quiz or trivia, from Etermax, Zynga, Duolingo or academic sources.
- I found no data on what share of async challenges are never answered in Trivia Crack or Words With Friends.

## 3. Invite and viral loops: how these games used friend challenges to grow (incl. WhatsApp and links in Brazil), and what users found spammy

### Takeaway
Trivia Crack's growth from Argentina across Latin America to the US was largely organic. It came from social features: Facebook sharing, sending and requesting free spins, friend challenges, and user-generated, country-specific questions credited by name. Its sharing prompts were described as "aggressive." Duolingo's own data says the hardest step in the social funnel is the very first invite. Link-based rooms (Gartic) are the browser-native viral pattern Brazilians already know. I found little direct user commentary on what feels spammy.

### Cited Findings
- Trivia Crack's viral mechanics as of January 2015:
  - The game pushes sharing "with almost militant vigor," including prompts to post level completions to Facebook.
  - Players send and request free spins, mostly through Facebook Connect.
  - Mobile players are nudged to the Facebook version.
  - Users submit questions, mostly country-specific, credited with the submitter's name.
  - Players rate questions "boring" or "fun."
  - Etermax's CEO reported about 1M active questions, 50M inactive, and about 2,000 added daily.
  - Adoption spread through Latin America in three waves, with the US Hispanic community as a "bridge" to the US.
  **[OLD]** — [Mobile Dev Memo, Jan 26 2015](https://mdm.co/trivia-crack-case-study-virality)
- Press coverage (as summarized in search results; pages not fetched):
  - Etermax said it "hasn't needed to advertise." Growth was "purely viral and organic," and early Facebook ad tests ran only in Italy and Germany.
  - An Etermax engineer called user-generated content "the main feature that allowed us to go viral."
  - The game reportedly peaked at about 25M DAU.
  **[OLD, UNVERIFIED]** — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/trivia-crack-global-sensation-buenos-aires); [PocketGamer.biz: growth](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/trivia-crack-growth/); [App Developer Magazine, 2017](https://appdevelopermagazine.com/Building-a-viral-app-platform/)
- Duolingo, Sep 2024: in the six-step Friend Streak funnel, "the biggest hurdle is sending the initial invite." A 1% gain there reaches far more learners than optimizing for "super friends" who invite dozens of people. — [Duolingo Blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/product-lessons-friend-streak/)
- As of June 2023, Duolingo's friend discovery used the profile tab and **contact sync**. Its post does not describe invite links. — [Duolingo Blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/friends-social-features/)
- Gartic Phone's loop is "create room, then Convidar, then copy link." There is no install and it runs in the browser. — [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/games/como-jogar-gartic-phone-telefone-sem-fio/)
- In 2025, a Brazilian brand (BIS) ran an RPG playable inside WhatsApp with no download. The brand reports more than 90,000 participants in three days. These are self-reported award-entry figures, and the split between paid and organic growth is unknown. The same entry claims Brazil has more than 165M WhatsApp users (over 80% of the population). — [Clio Awards entry](https://clios.com/winners-gallery/details/199871)
- Kahoot users complain about inappropriate nicknames and weak moderation in open-join games. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/156399/Kahoot/reviews/); [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/remote-work/kahoot-profile/)

### Inferences
- For a Brazilian PWA, the strongest native loop is likely a **WhatsApp deep link carrying a challenge**: "Acertei 8/10 em 'Song X' — me vence?" The recipient plays instantly in the browser with no install or sign-up, and only creates an account to save the result or reply. This combines Gartic's no-install link with Trivia Crack's friend challenge.
- Name-credited, country-specific content (Trivia Crack) suggests a lever for a song app: user-suggested songs or gap choices credited to the user.
- Treat the first invite as the critical funnel step (Duolingo). Prompt it at a moment of pride, such as a high score or a level-up, not at launch.
- To avoid spam:
  - The user always presses share; never auto-send.
  - Don't reward mass invites; rewards for spamming contacts create "Facebook-era" fatigue.
  - Cap reminder messages.
  - Never require invites to progress.

### Gaps
- I found no first-hand user complaints (reviews or Reddit) about Trivia Crack or Duolingo invite spam. "Aggressive" is an analyst's characterization.
- I found no published k-factor or invite-conversion data for Trivia Crack, Gartic, or any WhatsApp-driven game in Brazil.
- I could not determine whether Perguntados currently supports WhatsApp challenge links.

## 4. Ads: what players say about rewarded, interstitial and banner ads and ad frequency, misleading or unskippable ads, accepted ad-free prices, and sentiment and retention data

### Takeaway
The evidence consistently shows that **opt-in rewarded video is the most accepted format, and interstitials are the most hated and cause the most churn**. Banners sit in between. User anger concentrates on ads that are forced, ads after every turn or answer, ads during timed play, long or hard-to-close creatives, and ads that persist after paying for ad-free. Brazilian price anchors for ad removal are low: Perguntados charges R$ 2,90 per 7 days, and Duolingo Super annual works out to about R$ 15/month.

### Cited Findings

**Sentiment surveys (all vendor-run)**
- AdColony, 2017: rewarded video was rated "acceptable" by **40.1%**, interstitial display by only **12.0%**. The report's interstitial row reads 12.0% / 29.2% / 58.8%, which appears to be acceptable / neutral / unacceptable. **[OLD]** — [AdColony 2017](https://www.adcolony.com/blog/2017/10/03/measuring-mobile-mood-consumers-share-sentiments-new-survey/)
- AdColony/YouGov, Mar 17 2021, a worldwide sample of more than 300 users:
  - Rewarded is the "most acceptable" format for 36%, playable for 27%, banner for 22%.
  - **27% call interstitial display "completely unacceptable."**
  - 85% are more likely to interact with rewarded video.
  - Trivia (80%) and board games (85%) are the game types that make respondents happiest.
  **[OLD]** — [YouGov](https://yougov.com/articles/34758-rewarded-ads-are-most-acceptable-format); [AdColony 2021](https://www.adcolony.com/blog/2021/03/04/consumers-share-sentiments-on-mobile-apps-and-ads/)
- Unity survey (undated, probably mid-2010s): **46%** of players prefer incentivized video, versus full-screen pop-ups (29%), banners (20%) and standard interstitial video (5%). **[OLD]** — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/unity-the-majority-of-mobile-gamers-are-open-to-engaging-with-incentivized-video-ads/)
- Tapjoy studies, cited by ironSource in 2022: gamers "prefer rewarded ads to interstitials 4-to-1." **[OLD]** — [Unity/ironSource blog](https://unity.com/blog/understanding-the-impact-of-rewarded-ads-on-iap-retention-and-engagement)

**Retention and churn data**
- Unity AdQuality data for November 2025, published by GameBiz Consulting in PocketGamer.biz on Mar 24, 2026, across casual and midcore titles (sample sizes not stated):
  - "Interstitial ads consistently drive higher churn than rewarded videos."
  - D1 churn from rewarded ads was mostly under 2% on Android and under 1% on iOS.
  - D1 churn from interstitials reached about 10% on Android and more than 15% on iOS (AppLovin); one iOS interstitial game hit 22% D1.
  - Churn differed by up to **15x between ad networks**. Chartboost and Google AdMob had the lowest churn.
  - Average ad length: rewarded videos more than **60 seconds**; interstitials more than 15s on Android and 20s on iOS.
  - Creatives are getting longer and harder to exit, and store redirects are "mostly forced."
  - Churn was highest in a hyper-casual game.
  — [PocketGamer.biz, Mar 2026](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/what-our-data-reveals-about-ad-quality-and-player-churn-in-mobile-games/)
- ironSource (Unity), Aug 24 2022, eight high-DAU apps, correlational:
  - Users who completed one rewarded ad in their first week had at least **50% D30 retention**, against a 13% benchmark. Retention rose to 53–68% as rewarded views increased.
  - Daily sessions rose 34% after the first rewarded ad.
  - Users who engaged with rewarded ads were about 4.5x more likely to make a first purchase.
  - It recommends discoverable placements, such as a home or store button, and introducing ads in the first few sessions.
  - Selection bias is likely: engaged users both watch ads and stay.
  **[OLD]** — [Unity blog](https://unity.com/blog/understanding-the-impact-of-rewarded-ads-on-iap-retention-and-engagement)
- ZiMAD (Unity case study, undated): adding interstitials to rewarded video and banners raised revenue **17%** and ARPDAU **18%**, with a **2.5% drop in D7 retention**. — [Unity: ZiMAD](https://unity.com/en/resources/zimad-dig-out)
- An ad vendor's blog claims a GameAnalytics study found high ad frequency can raise first-week churn "up to 35%." I could not locate the original. **[UNVERIFIED]** — [Applixir](https://www.applixir.com/?p=2833)

**What users say**
- Optional rewarded ads are tolerated, and forced ones push players away:
  - An app reviewer happily watches ads for "double rewards" but considered deleting the game over mandatory ads.
  - A Gems of War forum post objects to ads that become *required* to keep pace.
  - In 2018, The Sims Mobile drew backlash for requiring an ad to get bonus currency, with no option to take the standard reward.
  — [JustUseApp: TDS reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6480174499/tds-tower-destiny-survive/reviews); [Gems of War forum](https://community.gemsofwar.com/t/rewarded-ads-a-break-down-for-the-4-9-update/62644); [SimsCommunity, Nov 2018](https://simscommunity.info/2018/11/27/on-top-of-everything-the-sims-mobile-now-forces-players-to-watch-ads/)
- Players respond to forced between-level ads with ad blockers, which also kill the rewarded ads they would have watched (r/AndroidGaming, Jul 2024). — [Reddit mirror](https://cal1.lr.ggtyler.dev/r/AndroidGaming/comments/1dzpy9v/in_case_you_didnt_know)
- Revive and second-chance offers are often designed so watching the ad looks like the obviously better deal (an industry guide). — [Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/rewarded-video-ad-placements)
- In trivia and word duels specifically, complaints cluster around:
  - A full-page ad after every answer (Trivia Crack).
  - Ads between questions in a 10-second timed mode (Perguntados BR).
  - Ads after every turn that sometimes won't close (Words With Friends).
  - "Overwhelming frequency and length" (WWF2).
  - Ads still appearing in the ad-free version (Trivia Crack reviewer).
  — [WhistleOut](https://www.whistleout.com/CellPhones/Apps/trivia-crack-app-review); [App Store BR](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/perguntados/id651510680); [App Store WWF](https://apple.co/3du8CJg); [Common Sense: WWF2](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/words-with-friends-2/user-reviews/adult)

### Inferences
- For this product, **rewarded-only** (opt-in, clearly labeled, never gating the core learning loop) matches what users accept. Good reward hooks:
  - An extra life or retry on a song.
  - Double XP for the session.
  - Unlocking a bonus song early.
  - A "hint" (reveal a letter).
- Do not put rewarded ads in the middle of a timed gap-fill. Offer them only at natural breaks: after a round, or before a duel starts.
- If interstitials are ever added, the 2025 Unity data suggests a measurable churn cost, especially on iOS and depending on the network. Limit them to round transitions, cap their frequency, and never show them to new users.
- Ad-free must be truly ad-free. Ads after purchase are a recurring review complaint.
- Price inference for Brazil:
  - Perguntados' R$ 2,90/week ad removal is about R$ 12/month.
  - Duolingo's annual Super is about R$ 15/month and bundles much more.
  - An ad-free tier around R$ 5–10/month (or an annual equivalent) would sit below both anchors. This is an inference, not tested data.
- Rewarded ads average more than 60s. Long rewarded videos may frustrate users if the reward is small. Size the reward to the wait.

### Gaps
- There is no independent (non-vendor) or recent (2024–2026) consumer survey on ad-format acceptability, and none specific to Brazil or to learning apps.
- There is no data on willingness to pay for ad-free tiers in Brazilian language or learning apps.
- I could not verify the GameAnalytics "35% churn" claim.
- I have no Reclame Aqui data on misleading ads in trivia or learning apps.

## 5. Web/PWA specifics: ad options for HTML5 web games (H5 Games Ads / Ad Placement API, AdSense, Ad Manager rewarded web), their requirements, and typical eCPM in Brazil vs the US

### Takeaway
Three Google paths exist for rewarded ads in a web game or PWA:
1. **H5 Games Ads (Ad Placement API)**: interstitial and rewarded inside HTML5 games. A closed beta that needs an approved AdSense account; approval is not guaranteed.
2. **Ad Manager rewarded ads for web (GPT)**: desktop, mobile and tablet web. The publisher builds the opt-in and the reward. There is no server-side verification on web.
3. **AdSense rewarded options**: Offerwall "rewarded ad" choice and rewarded ad units in beta, aimed at content-gating.

Reliable Brazil-vs-US eCPM numbers are scarce. The available signals put Brazil several times below the US.

### Cited Findings

**H5 Games Ads / Ad Placement API (Google)**
- Requirements: "You need an approved AdSense account to show ads in your H5 games." Access is by application form, and "Account approval is not guaranteed as it is subject to partner eligibility." It serves "interstitial and rewarded ads in your HTML5 games." The page was last updated 2026-06-18. — [Google Developers: Ad Placement beta](https://developers.google.com/ad-placement/docs/beta)
- Eligibility: any publisher that owns an H5 games website can apply if it follows the AdSense program policies. Approval grants limited permission for full-screen and rewarded ads that are "typically restricted for AdSense publishers." Without approval, code may misbehave and the account may face policy issues. — [AdSense: H5 Games Ads](https://adsense.google.com/start/h5-games-ads/); [pub.dev google_adsense docs](https://pub.dev/documentation/google_adsense/latest/topics/H5%20Games%20Ads-topic.html)
- Policy (Google Publisher Policies):
  - Runs "in web browsers and also in WebViews on native apps." For app WebViews you own, the AdMob integration (Android only) is "the only way to do this in a high-performing and policy compliant way."
  - Formats: interstitial and rewarded, with display plus TrueView/Bumper video.
  - Full-screen ads must use `adBreak()`.
  - **Not allowed:** ads before the app opens or after it closes; ads after the user closes another full-screen ad; unexpected ads during content; ads "after every user interaction"; ads that interrupt "continuous game play and heavy user interaction"; ads that interfere with navigation.
  - **Allowed:** ads between screens or content pages and at catalog-to-game transitions.
  - The API has frequency controls, but the page states no numeric limits.
  — [Google Publisher Policies: H5 games](https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/11975916)
- Rewarded H5 ads may be served only after the user explicitly chooses to view them, and rewards must have no value outside the publisher-user relationship (Google best-practice infographic). — [Google H5 policy infographic (PDF)](https://services.google.com/fh/files/helpcenter/h5_games_policy_best_practices_infographic.pdf)

**Ad Manager rewarded ads for web (GPT)**
- Runs on "desktop, mobile, and tablet web inventory," with mobile-optimized pages (viewport meta tag).
- Publisher setup:
  - Disable the "Block non-instream video ads" protection.
  - Build the opt-in screen and deliver the reward yourself.
  - Don't show a rewarded prompt for a non-rewarded ad.
  - Users who close early must confirm they forfeit the reward.
  - A display ad must be in view at least **7 seconds** to grant the reward.
  - You can't request multiple rewarded ads at once.
  - Filled by video and display demand by default.
  — [Google Ad Manager Help](https://support.google.com/admanager/answer/9116812)
- A search summary of the same page cited a 3-second in-view threshold, which **conflicts** with the 7 seconds on the page I fetched. Trust the fetched page and re-check before implementing. — [Google Ad Manager Help](https://support.google.com/admanager/answer/9116812)
- GPT events: `rewardedSlotReady`, then `makeRewardedVisible()`, then `rewardedSlotGranted` (listen to this one; some demand grants the reward before the ad ends), then `rewardedSlotClosed`. Server-side verification is app-only and unavailable on web, per a search summary of Google docs and a third-party guide. **[partly UNVERIFIED]** — [Google Publisher Tag sample](https://developers.google.com/publisher-tag/samples/display-rewarded-ad?hl=en); [Ezoic guide](https://www.ezoic.com/blog/setting-up-rewarded-ads-in-web-apps-and-websites-a-technical-guide)

**AdSense rewarded options for web (from search summaries; pages not fetched)**
- Offerwall offers a "Rewarded ad" choice: visitors watch a short ad to access content. It needs no code changes and can be targeted by URL. It is designed for content-gating, not game rewards.
- AdSense rewarded ad units (beta) are full-page image or video ads with a 5–30 second countdown; closing early forfeits the reward. Rewards can be premium content, ad-free pages or coupons.
- The publisher is solely responsible for granting rewards and must not imply that Google verifies them.
- **[UNVERIFIED in detail]** — [AdSense Help: Offerwall](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/15405364); [AdSense Help: rewarded](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/12726063)

**eCPM: Brazil vs US (weak data)**
- Appodeal Q4 2024 report: LATAM and MEA have "lower eCPMs but potential for volume-driven revenue." The US leads iOS rewarded video, followed by Saudi Arabia ($17.54) and Taiwan ($15.62). Brazil's chart values could not be extracted from the PDF. — [Appodeal Q4 2024 PDF](https://appodeal.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Quarterly_Mobile_eCPM_Report_-_Q4_2024.pdf); [Appodeal blog](https://appodeal.com/blog/mobile-ecpm-report-app-ad-monetization-worldwide-performance)
- A vendor glossary gives rough anchors of about **$10–25** for US iOS rewarded video and about **$1–3** for Brazil Android. Weakly sourced. **[UNVERIFIED]** — [MWM glossary](https://mwm.ai/glossary/ecpm)
- In MonetizeMore's 2024–25 insights, Brazil app-open ads grew through 2024 to a $1.10 eCPM peak in December. App-open ads are a different format from rewarded. — [MonetizeMore eCPM insights 2024-25](https://www.monetizemore.com/es/blog/insights-de-ecpm-2024-25-es/)
- An older, undated analysis found US iOS rewarded eCPMs fell 20–40% while Brazil was comparatively stable over the same months. **[undated]** — [Global Games Forum](https://www.globalgamesforum.com/features/how-is-your-ecpm-doing-a-six-month-trend-analysis)
- MonetizeMore, Sep 2023, explains why Brazilian CPMs are lower: purchasing power, a less mature and less competitive ad market, lower digital ad investment, and English-speaking markets paying the most. It gives no actual BR/US numbers. — [MonetizeMore PT-BR](https://www.monetizemore.com/pt-br/blog/cpm-estados-unidos-e-brasil/)
- Web-portal alternatives: a vendor guide says Poki splits 50/50 on Poki-sourced traffic and pays 100% on the developer's own traffic. CrazyGames' 2026 jam terms give developers 60% of in-game ad revenue. Typical earnings are "$200–$2,000/month" for a well-performing casual game, a rough unsourced estimate. **[UNVERIFIED]** — [Cinevva guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/web-game-monetization)

### Inferences
- For a PWA that is also an English-learning app, H5 Games Ads eligibility is uncertain. Google frames it for "H5 games websites," and approval is discretionary. The song game must clearly be the main content, and the plan should not depend on approval.
- A practical stack:
  - Apply to H5 Games Ads early.
  - In parallel, evaluate Ad Manager rewarded web. This usually requires an Ad Manager account, often obtained through a Google MCM partner, which is my assumption rather than a sourced fact.
  - Keep the ad-free tier as a revenue hedge.
- Web rewarded ads have no server-side verification. The client-side `rewardedSlotGranted` event must be trusted, so rewards should be low-value cosmetic or convenience items: lives, hints, double XP. Never anything that affects ranked fairness.
- The H5 policy bans ads "after every user interaction" and during "continuous game play." This matches what users say they hate, so following the policy and good UX point the same way.
- If the PWA is later wrapped as an Android app (TWA/WebView), Google says the AdMob-for-WebView integration is the compliant route.
- With Brazilian eCPMs plausibly 3–10x below the US, rewarded-only revenue per Brazilian user will be small. The ad-free or premium tier and, for growth, organic WhatsApp virality matter more. This is an inference from weak eCPM data.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable 2024–2026 rewarded eCPM figures for Brazil vs the US, for either in-app or web. The Statista and Appodeal Brazil values were masked or inside images. The H5 Games Ads and Ad Manager rewarded-web eCPMs by country are not published.
- H5 Games Ads approval criteria (traffic thresholds, whether educational or quiz sites qualify, PWA/TWA specifics) are not stated in the docs I fetched.
- I found no data on rewarded-ad fill rates for Brazilian mobile-web traffic.
- I did not fetch the AdSense rewarded-unit help pages to confirm the beta status and the 5–30s countdown.
