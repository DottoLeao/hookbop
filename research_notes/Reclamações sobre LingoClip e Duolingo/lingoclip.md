# LingoClip (formerly LyricsTraining): product, business model, and what real users praise and complain about

Research date: 2026-10-21. Scope: LingoClip apps (iOS/Android), its web PWA (lingoclip.app) and its legacy web site (lyricstraining.com). Review evidence comes mostly from Google Play (US and Brazil storefronts) and the Apple App Store (US and Brazil). Reddit, Trustpilot, Product Hunt, TikTok and YouTube could not be used (see Gaps).

**How the review sample was built (applies to all review stats below):** I pulled Google Play reviews for package `com.elasthink.lyricstraining` through the same public endpoint the listing's "See all reviews" page uses, for the `pt/BR` and `en/US` storefronts: newest 150 per country (BR: 2025-11-28 to 2026-10-19; US: 2025-04-08 to 2026-10-15), "most relevant" 150 per country, and up to 100 each of 1-star, 2-star and 3-star reviews per country. After de-duplication that gives **966 unique reviews with text** (339 rated 1-2 stars, 201 rated 3 stars, 426 rated 4-5 stars), dated 2017-2026. The sample over-weights negative reviews on purpose, so percentages are only meaningful *within* a rating band, not as a share of all users. Themes were counted by keyword matching (Portuguese and English), so they are approximate and can overlap. Individual Play reviews below are cited to the storefront listing they came from, with date, star rating and "helpful" votes (t = thumbs-up count). Reviews are paraphrased, not quoted.

## 1. What exactly does LingoClip offer, and how does its business model work?

### Takeaway
LingoClip is the rebranded LyricsTraining (renamed December 2022), made by Elasthink S.L. (Spain). It is a gap-fill game over YouTube music videos, with three modes (Choice, Type, Karaoke), four difficulty levels, per-song leaderboards, friend challenges, shareable playlists, levels, achievements and a daily streak. It is a freemium subscription app with **no ads in the apps**. Free users get **3 games a day**: a limit the server sets, which was "3 games every 30 minutes" until about 2022. Free users also get a small daily cap on translations. Premium costs **R$16,90-19,90/month or R$99,90-119,90/year in Brazil** and **$4.99-9.99/month or $29.99-59.99/year in the US**. The old lyricstraining.com site is still online, carries ads and stays more open.

You can compete with users from your country or the world, create friends-only challenges, and see your activity history. — [Google Play US](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elasthink.lyricstraining&hl=en_US&gl=US)

### Inferences
- Because the free limit is server-configurable, Elasthink can tighten it without shipping an update. That would explain why users report different limits: 1 song, 2 songs, waits of "12 minutes", "1:30h", "20h" or "24h". Users read it as an opaque, shifting paywall.
- Each plan having two price points per store suggests price testing, legacy and new SKUs, or a discounted offer. Comparing with the older aggregator listing ($6.99 → $9.99 monthly, $41.99 → $59.99 annual) suggests the list price rose over time. This is not confirmed.
- The catalog depends on YouTube embeds of official videos (VEVO and similar), which can be blocked from embedding or blocked by region. The Apple MusicKit player looks like a hedge against that. The "licensing costs" justification is the developer's own claim, and I could not verify that they hold music licences.
- The business model is subscription-only in the apps, with no rewarded ads. The free web site is monetized with display ads. That leaves a gap for a free product that lets users unlock extra plays with rewarded ads, which several users explicitly ask for (see Q3).

### Gaps
- No official pricing or FAQ page exists: lingoclip.com/premium and /faq return 404. I could not confirm what the two price points per plan mean, whether a free trial exists, or the current exact translation cap.
- I could not read the Google Play in-app purchase price range in BRL.
- The size of the song catalog (number of songs per language) is not published anywhere I found.

## 2. What do users praise most?

### Takeaway
Users praise the **fun, game-like listening practice** with real songs and videos. Reviewers call it addictive and more fun than Duolingo or Babbel. They also praise **adjustable difficulty**, the absence of **ads** in the app, a simple interface, karaoke mode, the replay-line control, and **responsive song-request support**. Many free users accept 3 games a day as a fair sample. Teachers like it as a classroom supplement.

### Cited Findings

- Teachers and educators: TESL Ontario lists it as an ESL resource for warm-ups and homework (search snippet). ### Inferences
- The things people love are the **real-time listening challenge** (time pressure plus replaying the line), **choosing music you like**, and an ad-free, low-friction flow. The perfection-chasing score loop (wanting to reach 100%) is the hook. Ranks and duels are rarely mentioned as reasons to love the app.
- Fast, human song-request handling turns critics into 5★ reviewers. A competitor without that capacity needs scalable catalog growth, such as community-synced lyrics.

### Gaps
- Without star-level sentiment tooling I can't say exactly how often each feature is praised. The percentages are keyword-based.

## 3. What do users complain about most, and how common is each complaint?

### Takeaway
In 1-2★ reviews the top complaints are: (1) **technical failures**: videos not loading or freezing, crashes, login loops (about 25%, concentrated in 2020-2021); (2) **paywall, price and the 3-games limit** (about 19% mention paying or subscription, about 13% the limit or the wait). Since 2024 the limit and paywall clearly dominate (about 22% each of 1-3★). Next come (3) **no full-sentence translation, so users feel it doesn't teach and is just karaoke**; (4) **catalog gaps or dated catalog**; (5) **YouTube "video unavailable" errors**; (6) **romanized-only Japanese and Korean**; (7) **difficulty and UX** (lyrics too fast for beginners, Type mode too strict, tutorial problems). Lyrics sync or accuracy errors are rarer (about 1-5%) but collect many helpful votes. Social features hardly come up (about 1%).

### Inferences
- Complaint mix by era: 2020-2021 was dominated by stability problems and the 30-minute wait. 2022 onward is dominated by the tighter 3-per-day paywall, catalog freshness, YouTube embed failures and the lack of real translation or learning scaffolding.
- Rewarded ads come up spontaneously in at least 6 reviews from 2020-2026 in both BR and US. That directly supports a model of "free, plus optional rewarded ads for extra plays".
- Users treat **full-line translation, slow-down playback and vocabulary review** as table stakes for "learning". Without them the product reads as karaoke. These are cheap features for a competitor to differentiate on.
- Romanization complaints don't matter for Brazilians learning English. Catalog freshness (current hits, K-pop, gospel, classics, Disney) and per-artist depth matter a lot in Brazil.
- The developer's heavy solicitation of rating changes probably inflates the star averages, so complaint *volume* is likely understated by the ratings.

- Keyword coding is approximate: "erro" or "load" can over-count bugs, and paraphrased complaints are missed. A manual coding pass over the 966-review dataset would sharpen the percentages.
- I couldn't quantify the iOS App Store complaint mix. Only about 10 US App Store reviews were visible, and the BR App Store page showed 4 distinct reviews.

## 4. Why do people churn or uninstall?

### Takeaway
The explicit reasons people give for uninstalling are mainly: **(a) the paywall or limit hits too early** (3 a day, sometimes 1-2, or long opaque waits); **(b) technical failures**: freezes, videos that won't play, login loops (mostly 2020-2022); **(c) favorite or current songs missing**; **(d) the sense that it doesn't teach**: no translation, too fast for beginners, YouTube subtitles do the same; **(e) script and feature regressions**: romaji or romanized Korean, the April 2026 removal of lyric copying, removed teacher and challenge features.

### Cited Findings
- Explicit uninstall or "not keeping it" mentions in the sample: **24 reviews**. Breakdown by stated reason (my coding): bugs, freezes or login about 11; paywall or limit about 7; missing current songs 1 (BTS, 2026); removed copy-lyrics 1 (2026); romanized Korean 1 (2025); quizzes turned free time into a chore 1 (2023). — [Google Play BR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elasthink.lyricstraining&hl=pt_BR&gl=BR); [Google Play US](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elasthink.lyricstraining&hl=en_US&gl=US)
- Switching to a free competitor: BR (2023-09-08, 2★, t8) uninstalled because the app blocks access until you pay. They moved to another app with the same method that is genuinely free so far, identified only by a big "S" logo. — [Google Play BR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elasthink.lyricstraining&hl=pt_BR&gl=BR)
- Paywall churn examples: BR 2019-09-25 (2★, uninstalled), BR 2019-01-27 (1★, felt offended, uninstalling), BR 2021-02-16 (3★, t2: remembered why they uninstalled years ago, too limited, better to use the website), BR 2023-12-22 (3★, paywall on the 2nd song, uninstalling), US 2026-05-31 (2★, not keeping it). — same sources
- Bug churn examples: BR 2020-10-02 (1★, t3), 2020-11-30 (1★: will reinstall and give 5★ if fixed), 2020-12-01, 2020-12-10, 2021-12-21 (password), 2022-06-05 (won't open). — [Google Play BR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elasthink.lyricstraining&hl=pt_BR&gl=BR)
- Content and regression churn: BR 2026-07-09 (no new BTS songs, uninstalling); BR 2026-04-19 (can't copy lyrics after the update, uninstalling); US 2025-12-05 (romanized Korean, deleting); BR 2024-03-23 (will come back if Hangul is added). — [Google Play BR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elasthink.lyricstraining&hl=pt_BR&gl=BR); [Google Play US](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elasthink.lyricstraining&hl=en_US&gl=US)
- Value doubt: two US reviewers say YouTube with subtitles does the same job, so the app isn't needed (US 2025-03-07, t3; US 2025-07-12, t4). Another will stop using it unless in-app translation becomes free (US 2024-01-11, t12). — [Google Play US](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elasthink.lyricstraining&hl=en_US&gl=US)
- Habit loss: a Brazilian user asks for a streak-restore option because life gets in the way and you can't always open the app (2026-03-05, 1★). A BR user complains that the 24-hour rolling wait doesn't reset at midnight, which breaks a routine set at a variable time (2023-06-15, 3★, t1). — [Google Play BR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elasthink.lyricstraining&hl=pt_BR&gl=BR)
- Teacher churn: removal of the custom-task creator (US 2026-07-15, 1★) and of the multi-person challenge-link ranking (BR 2022-08-09, 1★). A BR teacher notes that the website's gap-creation tool isn't in the app (2023-01-27, 4★). — [Google Play US](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elasthink.lyricstraining&hl=en_US&gl=US); [Google Play BR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elasthink.lyricstraining&hl=pt_BR&gl=BR)

### Inferences
- The **session ceiling** of 3 games, about 10-15 minutes, is the structural churn driver for engaged free users in price-sensitive markets like Brazil. Users want to binge a favorite artist, and the paywall stops them right when motivation peaks.
- Teachers who assign the app to students are a distribution channel. When teacher tools or YouTube embeds break, whole classrooms are exposed to the failure (BR 2025-02-25 school-assignment complaint).
- The free web site with ads cannibalizes the app and confuses users, who ask why the app isn't free when the site is.

### Gaps
- No retention or DAU data is public (I found no Sensor Tower or data.ai figures). Churn reasons come only from reviews, which are biased toward vocal users.
- I could not reach Reddit (search returned no threads, and direct Reddit API access was blocked). Forum discussion of why people stop using it remains unverified.

### Inferences
- Brazilians learning English through music are already LingoClip's strongest audience. The gaps they name are the R$17-20/month subscription, a 3-games-a-day ceiling, stale or narrow catalogs for Brazilian tastes, no Portuguese line translation or explanation, and no beginner slow mode. A free PWA with rewarded ads, Portuguese-first explanations, fresh charts plus gospel, K-pop and classics, and a beginner mode would target these directly.
- The "big S" free app a Brazilian switched to in 2023 is plausibly Sounter, which appears as a related app in the BR App Store. This is unverified.
- Teachers and influencers (Bi e Gi; school assignments) drive acquisition in Brazil. Teacher and classroom features (multi-student challenge links, class rankings) were valued and then removed, which leaves an opening.
